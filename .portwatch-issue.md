### New effort candidate: CoolFox

**Repo:** https://github.com/yemao2333/c-line
**Evidence sheet:** https://github.com/yemao2333/c-line/blob/main/EVIDENCE.md

`cool_fox`, a build on VIctory's AMD branch, running the Xbox Series build through the HLE layer. It reaches the open world with a lit scene, textures and traffic, and holds 4,180–4,200 draws per frame.

Measured on **RTX 3060 Ti 8 GB**, i5-12400F, 31.8 GB RAM, 1440p. Filter `Ms > 40 && Draws >= 200`, 600 s window after `draws >= 1000`:

| rep | window start | world samples | median ms/frame | fps | median draws | peak private |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| z3-tight-1 | 69 s | 101 | 246.3 | 4.06 | 4180 | 17.63 GB |
| z3-tight-2 | 80 s | 101 | 243.9 | 4.10 | 4193 | 17.66 GB |
| z3-tight-3 | 68 s | 101 | 240.2 | 4.16 | 4200 | 18.35 GB |

For reference, the upstream fork `fxceless/victory-fork` measured the same way on the same machine: 522.2 / 241.2 / 220.0 ms across three reps.

**What is not claimed:** story progress, mission completion and render quality are not assessed. Only frame time, draw count and memory were measured, on one machine, on NVIDIA only.
